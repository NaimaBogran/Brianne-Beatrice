# Brianne Beatrice — Professional Portfolio

A custom portfolio website designed and developed for **Brianne Beatrice**, an award-winning educator, director, actor, and public speaker.

🔗 **Live Site:** [briannebeatrice.com](https://briannebeatrice.com)

## About the Project

Brianne needed a professional online presence that could bring together the many sides of her career in one place.

Rather than creating a traditional portfolio centered around a single role, the goal was to build a site that could showcase her work across **education, directing, acting, and public speaking** while still feeling cohesive and easy to navigate.

The result is a responsive, content-focused portfolio that gives Brianne a central place to share her experience, accomplishments, media features, and work.

## ✨ Features

* Responsive design across desktop, tablet, and mobile
* Dedicated sections for teaching, directing, acting, and speaking
* Professional experience and career highlights
* Awards and recognition
* Production history and featured work
* Media, articles, and podcast appearances
* Testimonials and professional recognition
* Contact and booking information
* Custom branding and visual details
* Custom domain configuration and deployment

## 🎨 Design

The site was designed around Brianne's existing personal brand while giving her content room to stand out.

The visual direction combines a clean, professional layout with **black, white, and gold accents**, incorporating her signature branding throughout the experience.

Because Brianne works across several different areas, one of the biggest priorities was creating an information architecture that makes it easy for visitors to explore a specific part of her career without making the overall site feel disconnected.
