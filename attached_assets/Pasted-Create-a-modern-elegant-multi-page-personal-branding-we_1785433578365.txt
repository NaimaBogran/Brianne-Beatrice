Create a modern, elegant multi-page personal branding website prototype for a professional whose long-term goal is to become a nationally recognized motivational speaker.

This is NOT the final website. It is a clickable mockup/prototype that demonstrates the site's layout, navigation, user experience, and overall design. Use placeholder content, placeholder images, and sample text throughout. The goal is to present a polished concept that can later be filled with real content.

## About the Client

The client is an accomplished professional with many roles including:
- Community college professor specializing in acting and public speaking
- Theatre director
- Actor (do NOT make this the primary focus)
- Public speaker
- Aspiring motivational speaker

She has received numerous awards, recognitions, and community honors, has been featured in articles, has appeared on podcasts, and has an extensive professional background.

Her acting experience should be treated as part of her story and credibility, not the primary focus of the website.

The overall brand should position her as someone who inspires confidence, communication, leadership, storytelling, and personal growth.

Think of websites like Mel Robbins, Simon Sinek, or Brené Brown—not in terms of copying their design, but in creating a polished personal brand with a clear mission.

## Technical

Use React with TypeScript.

Organize components cleanly.

Separate reusable UI components.

Create a modern design system with reusable cards, buttons, typography, spacing, and color variables.

Use Tailwind CSS.

Use Framer Motion for tasteful animations.

Use Lucide React icons.

The code should be clean, modular, and easy to replace placeholder content later.

Focus on creating an impressive client presentation prototype rather than a content-complete website.
